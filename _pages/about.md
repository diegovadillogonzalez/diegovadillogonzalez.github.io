Hi there!

I’m Diego Vadillo González, a PhD student in the Social Sciences program at Universidad Pablo de Olavide in Seville, Spain. My research interests lie at the intersection of between social inequalities and educational systems internationally compared. 

My doctoral research compares how social inequalities are understood and addressed in primary education in Spain and Germany. It examines the perspectives of school professionals as well as the educational policies and regulations that shape each country’s education system. The research is conducted within the [Grupo de Investigación en Acción Socioeducativa](https://www.upo.es/investiga/gias/) at Universidad Pablo de Olavide.

I use a mixed-methods approach. My qualitative methods include structured and semi-structured interviews, fieldwork, non-participant observation, round-table discussions, and discourse analysis. For the quantitative component, I conduct text analysis using R.

I am also pursuing the [MA Research Training Program](https://www.sowi.hu-berlin.de/de/studium/studiengaenge/masterstudiengaenge/martp) at [Humboldt Universität zu Berlin](https://www.hu-berlin.de/en).
