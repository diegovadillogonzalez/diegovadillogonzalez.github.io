Hi there!

My name is Diego Vadillo González. I am a PhD Student in the program of Social Sciences at Universidad Pablo de Olavide, in Seville, Spain. The topics I am interested in are in the intersection between social inequalities and educational systems internationally compared. My doctor (mainly) compares how social inequalities are understood and confronted internationally in primary education systems, by the professionals working on the schools and by the educational regulations that shape the systems across countries. The countries where the research is carried out are Spain and Germany. It is conducted within the research group ["Grupo de Investigación en Acción Socioeducativa"](https://www.upo.es/investiga/gias/), from Universidad Pablo de Olavide.

The approach I use consists on a mixed methods one. As qualitative methods, I use scripted and half scripted interviews, fieldwork, non-participatory observation, round table and discourse analysis. As quantitative methods I use quantitative textanalysis using R.

Currently, I am also studying the [MA Research Training Program](https://www.sowi.hu-berlin.de/de/studium/studiengaenge/masterstudiengaenge/martp) at [Humboldt Universität zu Berlin](https://www.hu-berlin.de/en).
