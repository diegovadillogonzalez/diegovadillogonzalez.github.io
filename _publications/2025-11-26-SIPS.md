## Abstract
This study analyses the regulatory and policy framework governing the response to social inequalities in secondary education centres in Andalusia, Spain. Drawing on the fields of educational policy, social pedagogy, and inclusion studies, the paper examines the legal instruments, institutional practices, and educational measures that shape schools’ capacity to address social disadvantage. The analysis highlights the importance of aligning regulatory provisions with inclusive and socially responsive educational practices in order to reduce structural inequalities and promote equitable opportunities for all students.
